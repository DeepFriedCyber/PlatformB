# PlatformB Product Vision v0.01

## 1. What PlatformB Is

PlatformB is a B2B discovery and research platform.

It helps professionals find, understand and compare companies, products,
services and industry information using natural-language search and
verifiable sources.

It also helps companies make useful information about their products,
capabilities and expertise easier to discover.

Initial domains:

- Cybersecurity
- Artificial Intelligence
- Cybersecurity + AI

PlatformB is not intended to be another personal social network.


## 2. Core Principle

Relevance should beat popularity.

A company should not need a large follower count, high posting frequency
or large advertising budget to be discovered for a requirement it can
genuinely satisfy.

PlatformB connects user intent with relevant company information and
supporting evidence.


## 3. The Two Sides of PlatformB

### Discover

For professionals researching the market.

PlatformB helps users:

- find relevant companies and products;
- search using natural language;
- understand what companies actually provide;
- compare alternatives;
- inspect evidence supporting claims;
- identify information that cannot be established;
- discover useful research and technical content;
- save useful research.

### Be Discovered

For companies.

PlatformB helps companies:

- become discoverable for relevant buyer requirements;
- explain products and capabilities clearly;
- make existing reports, articles, videos and documentation discoverable;
- support important claims with evidence;
- understand what users are trying to discover;
- identify questions their public information does not answer;
- identify content and information gaps;
- improve their discoverability.

Payment must not change factual relevance ranking.


## 4. Search Is the Centre of the Product

Primary interface:

"Use natural language inputs to search and compare"

Example:

"We are a 200-person UK company using Microsoft 365 and Defender.
Which MDR providers should we consider?"

PlatformB should identify important elements of the request and make its
interpretation visible to the user.

Example:

- MDR
- UK
- SME
- Microsoft 365
- Microsoft Defender
- existing-tool coexistence

Users should be able to correct important interpretations.

PlatformB should clarify rather than silently assume when ambiguity
materially affects the result.


## 5. Evidence

PlatformB distinguishes between:

- information provided by a company;
- information supported by public sources;
- PlatformB-derived classifications;
- information that has not been established.

Unknown is a valid result.

PlatformB should prefer:

"Not established from available sources"

over generating a plausible answer.


## 6. Company Information

A PlatformB company presence is not primarily a marketing landing page.

It is a structured knowledge resource.

It may contain:

- overview;
- products and services;
- capabilities;
- integrations;
- supported environments;
- geographic coverage;
- target organisation sizes;
- industries;
- research;
- articles;
- videos;
- documentation;
- evidence;
- common questions;
- unanswered questions.


## 7. Content

PlatformB should not encourage companies to post simply to maintain
visibility.

Existing useful content should remain discoverable when relevant,
regardless of when it was published.

A detailed report may answer many different future searches.

PlatformB should help expose those answers without requiring users to
discover and read an entire report first.

The original source remains available for verification and deeper reading.


## 8. Company Discoverability

PlatformB should help a company understand:

- what people are searching for;
- which searches cause the company to appear;
- why it appears;
- which capabilities are well evidenced;
- which capabilities are unclear;
- questions users ask about the company;
- questions its public information cannot answer;
- useful existing content that is difficult to discover;
- subjects for which better information would improve discoverability.

The objective is not social engagement.

PlatformB should not optimise around:

- follower counts;
- likes;
- reposts;
- posting frequency;
- vanity engagement.


## 9. Initial Product Surfaces

PlatformB may eventually contain:

- Search
- Companies
- Learn
- Feed
- Workspace

Search is the initial centre of the product.

Learn, Feed and Workspace should only grow when they support the core
discovery and research workflow.


## 10. Individuals

PlatformB is not intended to reproduce LinkedIn-style personal profiles.

Individual identity should initially be lightweight:

- name;
- official job title where appropriate;
- organisation / verified affiliation;
- relevant contributions.

Personal branding and follower mechanics are outside the initial scope.


## 11. Initial Product Hypothesis

PlatformB should answer:

"Can we make finding, understanding and comparing B2B companies and
products materially easier than searching the web, browsing company
websites and consuming chronological social feeds?"

For companies:

"Can we make useful company information discoverable at the moment
somebody has a relevant requirement?"


## 12. Initial Case Study

Sophos is the first worked example.

Initial source material includes:

- Sophos + Microsoft solution material;
- Sophos AI Security 2026;
- 2026 CISO Report.

The case study demonstrates how existing company information can become
searchable, understandable and evidence-backed without requiring the
company to create another social post.


## 13. Product Philosophy

Understand first.
Retrieve second.
Verify third.

And:

Relevance over popularity.
Evidence over assertion.
Useful information over engagement.
Clarify rather than assume.
Unknown rather than invented.
